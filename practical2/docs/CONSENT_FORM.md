# Consent to Contribute a Language Sample

## What we are asking you for

We are asking you to share a short sentence, phrase, or proverb in
[Rukiga / Yoruba — delete as applicable] and to help us write an English
translation of it. We are university students building a small, original
dataset of written [Rukiga / Yoruba] text with English translations for a
data science course assignment.

## What we will do with it

- Your contributed text (and the English translation) will become part of
  a dataset that we plan to publish **openly**, under a
  **[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0) — proposed, to be
  confirmed by the authors]** license.
- Once published, anyone will be able to download, use, and share this
  dataset, including for research or commercial purposes, as long as they
  credit the dataset per the license.
- Your text may be used to help build or evaluate language tools (for
  example, translation or text-processing tools) for [Rukiga / Yoruba].

## What we will NOT do

- We will **not** record or publish your name, phone number, address,
  email, or any other detail that could identify you personally. We assign
  an anonymous code (like "C001") to your contribution instead.
- We will not publish anything you tell us is private, sensitive, or about
  another named person without their consent.

## Your participation is voluntary

- You can say no, or stop at any time, with no consequence to you.
- You can ask us to withdraw your contribution **at any time before the
  dataset is published**. Once the dataset is published openly, we may not
  be able to remove copies that others have already downloaded, so please
  let us know before then if you want to withdraw.
- You do not have to explain why you want to withdraw.
